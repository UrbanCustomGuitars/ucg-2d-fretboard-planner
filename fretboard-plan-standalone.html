Overhang
Overhang is the distance from the CENTRE of an outer string to the edge of the board. It is what stops that string sliding off. On a fanned board it is measured square across the neck, not along the slanted string.
Bass is the low E side. Treble is the high E side. Uneven numbers move the strings off the centre of the board, which is the point of them.
